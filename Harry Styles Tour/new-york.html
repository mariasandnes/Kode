PUBLIC TRANSPORT

Best way to get around Subway and walking The subway runs 24/7 and is the fastest way around the city. Tap-to-pay works everywhere. Walking is easy and safe in busy areas.
Airport → City center Take the AirTrain from JFK to Jamaica Station, then transfer to the subway (E, J, or Z lines). Much cheaper than Uber and avoids traffic. Remember to buy your ticket before getting on the train
City center → Venue Take any train to Penn Station (A, C, E, 1, 2, 3). Madison Square Garden is directly above the station Remember to tap in and out of the station.
