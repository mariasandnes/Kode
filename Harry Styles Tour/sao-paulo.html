Best way to get around Metro or Uber Metro is clean, fast, and safe. Uber is cheap, easy and safer than random taxis
Airport → Venue Uber: Fastes and easiest with luggage, takes about 1 hour Train + metro: Cheaper but slower. Take the airport train to the city (Line 13-Jade), transfer to metro system. Follow google maps Remember to buy train and metro tickets before entering the train.
